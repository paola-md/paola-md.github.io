Findings

A question, and what the paper actually found. Every claim is a result stated in that paper’s own abstract.

  1. Do students judge AI-generated feedback differently from human feedback?

    Students judged identical feedback as less credible, and lower quality, when they were told it came from AI.

    Who Gives Feedback Matters: Student Biases Towards Human and AI-Generated Formative Feedback · Journal of Computer Assisted Learning 2025

  2. Does knowing feedback came from AI change how students rate it?

    Students who could not tell who wrote the feedback rated the AI's higher; those who could tell preferred the human's.

    AI or Human? Evaluating Student Feedback Perceptions in Higher Education · European Conference on Technology Enhanced Learning (ECTEL 2024) 2024

  3. Does data storytelling make visualisations more effective?

    Data-storytelling annotations helped people find data points and grasp the main message, but did not help them complete higher-order analytical tasks. (128 participants, four visualisations.)

    "Piecing Data Connections Together Like a Puzzle": Effects of Increasing Task Complexity on the Effectiveness of Data Storytelling Enhanced Visualisations · CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI '25) 2025

  4. What predicts whether students trust AI-powered educational technology?

    Gender and educational background correlated significantly with how much students trusted AI-powered educational technology.

    The critical role of trust in adopting AI-powered educational technology for learning: An instrument for measuring student perceptions · Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence 2025

  5. How does teaching experience show up in classroom behaviour?

    Experienced teachers moved directly from lecturing into student interaction and then into real-time instructional adjustment; less experienced teachers did not.

    Behavioural transitions in team teaching · ASCILITE 2025

  6. What do acoustic patterns in teachers' speech reveal about team teaching?

    Loudness varied more in the speech of high-experience teachers, in undergraduate classes, and during collaborative tasks.

    AI-Driven Analytics of Team-Teaching Talk: Acoustic Patterns Across Experience, Cohorts and the Learning Design · Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2026

  7. Can a dashboard help co-teachers reflect on how they teach together?

    Seeing their own movement and interaction data helped co-teachers become more aware of how they actually teach together.

    TeamTeachingViz: Benefits, Challenges, and Ethical Considerations of Using a Multimodal Analytics Dashboard to Support Team Teaching Reflection · Learning Analytics and Knowledge (LAK '25) 2025

  8. Do teachers want the same learning-analytics dashboard online as in blended classrooms?

    What teachers want from a learning-analytics dashboard differs considerably between blended and online contexts — preferences, requirements and concerns all shift.

    Making machine learning findings accessible to teachers in blended classrooms · International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 2026

  9. Does giving learners a personalised worked example improve their writing?

    Retrieving a worked example matched to the learner's own draft improved both writing performance and experience, drawing on a database of over 180,000 recipes.

    Enhancing Procedural Writing Through Personalized Example Retrieval: A Case Study on Cooking Recipes · International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 2024

  10. Is it better to teach inquiry strategies together or one at a time?

    Four learner profiles emerged, and combining inquiry strategies supported conceptual learning better than using any one alone.

    Teaching and Measuring Multidimensional Inquiry Skills using Interactive Simulations · Lecture Notes in Computer Science (AIED 2024) 2024

  11. Do self-reports and trace data agree about self-regulated learning?

    What students say about their own self-regulated learning and what their trace data shows agree only weakly.

    Navigating Self-regulated Learning Dimensions: Exploring Interactions Across Modalities · Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2024

  12. What do teachers actually do when shown machine-learned student profiles?

    Showing teachers machine-learned learner profiles prompted concrete interventions and course changes, not just interest.

    Visualizing Self-Regulated Learner Profiles in Dashboards: Design Insights from Teachers · Communications in Computer and Information Science 2023

  13. Does adaptive writing support actually get learners to revise?

    Adaptive writing support measurably increased revision; learners improved over time, and women revised more efficiently.

    Understanding Revision Behavior in Adaptive Writing Support Systems for Education · Zenodo 2023

  14. Do student profiles transfer across different flipped-classroom courses?

    Multi-dimensional profiles held up across three different flipped-classroom courses, and the profiles differed significantly in academic performance.

    Identifying and Comparing Multi-dimensional Student Profiles Across Flipped Classrooms · Lecture Notes in Computer Science 2022

  15. Can self-regulated learning behaviour be tracked as it changes over time?

    Across 183 chef apprentices and more than 121,000 learning-journal entries, the method tracked how learning patterns changed over time and produced interpretable profiles.

    Evolutionary Clustering of Apprentices' Self-Regulated Learning Behavior in Learning Journals · IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies 2022
